How I work

The principles I apply to quality control and submission readiness.

Traceability

Every value in a submission links back to its source, so a reviewer can follow the path from form to dataset to documentation.

Clarity for reviewers

Documents are written for a reviewer who opens them without context, with data decisions explained plainly and consistently.

Early detection

Issues are cheapest to resolve at the form and specification stage, so I review early rather than at submission.

Standards-based consistency

CDISC conventions give sponsors and regulators a common reading of the data. I apply them consistently and document any deviation.
